Christmas 2024 Potluck  

On December 15th, our fraternity family gathered at the Goodyear recreation campus for our annual Christmas potluck. It was a beautiful afternoon filled with delicious food prepared by our members, warm fellowship, and the joy of reconnecting while discovering new facets of one another’s lives. Those unable to attend were deeply missed. The afternoon concluded with the heartfelt recitation of the Franciscan Rosary, a cherished tradition. As always, the remaining food was donated to those in need, with Kevin kindly volunteering to deliver it to St. Vincent de Paul.​

Rite of Admission for Barb Otterson, OFS

On Sunday, October 27, 2024 our fraternity joyfully joined in celebrating the profession of Barb Otterson, into the Secular Franciscan Order (OFS). Her decision to embrace our Rule speaks to her desire to bring the spirit of St. Francis-of humility, peace, and service-into her daily life, family, and community.

She would like to extend her heartfelt thanks to everyone who played a role in her formation journey. From her initial inquiry to her profession, each of you has shared your knowledge, guidance, and love, helping her grow in faith and prepare for this lifelong commitment. Your support has been invaluable in shaping her Franciscan vocation.

As we welcome Barb into our fraternity, let us continue to support her on this path, which calls for ongoing growth in faith, prayer, and action. We trust that her presence will enrich our fraternity, and that together, we will continue to reflect Francis’s love and care for all creation.

Meet Our New 2024 Council

Our fraternity held their Triennial Election Chapter during the June 23 gathering. Our presider was our Regional Minister Julianne Hartzell and our Pastoral Witness was Bill Neu.
Phillip Brocker was re-elected Minister for a second term, as was Jeff Persson as Formation Director. Trish Persson is now our Vice-Minister. Doralee Tayler will replace Trish as Secretary. Deacon Richard Carmody accepted to be elected Treasurer in place of Steve Roszhart. Phyllis Smith is our new Councilor-at-Large.
And last but not least, Rita Cummins is our new Councilor-JPIC replacing Cathy Roszhart. Deacon Ed Mirasol from San Damiano fraternity remains our Spiritual Assistant.
