PRAYER BOX

“Let each one confidently make known his need to another that the other might discover what is needed
and minister to him. Let each one love and care for his brother as a mother loves and cares for her son in those matters in which God has given him the grace.”
                                                                        ~ St. Francis of Assisi, The Earlier Rule, (IX: Begging Alms), 71
​​​​
  • For more vocations to the First, Second and Third Orders, especially our Secular Franciscan Order. May each one of us have the diligence and persistence to invite others to discover our Franciscan way of life.
  • For all the servant leaders of our Order that the Holy Spirit may help them guide and animate our fraternities.
  • For our aspirants Allyson, Obdulia, Sue and Thomas may they feel the grace of the Holy Spirit as
    they discern their calling into the Secular Franciscan Order.
  • For our candidates Doug, Tony, Anthony, Janet, Michael, and Deacon Mike, may they feel the grace of the Holy Spirit as they continue to discern their callings into the Secular Franciscan Order.
  • For all of the members of our OFS family who are struggling with health issues for themselves or their families, for those dealing with grief from the loss of a loved one or family member may they find comfort and strength.
  • For the repose of the souls of all our family members and friends who have gone before us.
  • For the repose of the souls of our Franciscan family members especially our brother Bill Rizzetto and our sisters Mary Bissonette and Louise Marquez.
  • For all who have wandered away from the Church especially our children, grandchildren and
    Godchildren.
  • For all the members of the Second Order, especially our "Desert Nuns", the Poor Clares of Perpetual
    Adoration at Our Lady of Solitude Monastery in Tonopah.
  • For all our aging parents and their caregivers. May God keep them and bless them.
  • For the lapsed members of our fraternity, may they find their way back to attend and participate in our monthly gatherings.
  • For all the special intentions collected in our Prayer Box during gatherings and in thanksgiving for all our prayers answered.
